Complete Children's Book Publishing Services

We cover every stage a children's book author needs, and we'll explain why each one matters rather than just listing it.

Manuscript assessment comes first, because knowing where your story actually stands — not just whether it's "good" — shapes every decision after it. Children's book editing and story editing differ from adult manuscript editing: pacing has to work in page turns, not chapters, and every unnecessary word competes with the illustrations for space. Proofreading then catches what editing misses before anything goes to print.

Illustration and character design matter because young readers connect with characters visually as much as through the story — a character who looks different from page to page breaks that connection fast. Cover design has to work as a thumbnail online and as a physical object on a shelf or in a classroom.

Interior layout and formatting decide how text and illustrations sit together on the page, and that changes across ebook, paperback and hardcover files. ISBN guidance keeps your book properly catalogued and searchable. Amazon KDP and print on demand let you reach readers without holding physical stock, and distribution and marketing support help people actually find your book once it's live.

As one of the children's book publishers for new authors Australia turns to for this full range, we handle every stage with children's books specifically in mind — not adapted from adult publishing processes.

Self publishing children's books in Australia has changed a lot over the past decade. Platforms like Amazon KDP mean you no longer need a traditional publishing deal to get your book into readers' hands — print on demand and ebook publishing put the process in your control.

Self publishing children's book Australia authors choose this route because it means owning your rights, keeping creative control, and getting to market faster.
